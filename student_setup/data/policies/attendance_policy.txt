Attendance Policy
1. A student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for the end-semester examination.
2. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a valid reason (medical or approved leave) and supporting documents.
3. Students below 65% attendance are not eligible for the end-semester examination.
4. Faculty mentors must counsel students whose attendance falls below 75% at the mid-semester review.
