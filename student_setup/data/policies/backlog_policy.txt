Backlog and Academic Support Policy
1. Students with more than two active backlogs are placed under academic monitoring.
2. Remedial classes are offered for subjects where a student has an internal average below 50%.
3. Academic counseling is recommended for students showing multiple indicators of difficulty.
4. Any decision affecting a student's academic status must be approved by the Head of Department.
